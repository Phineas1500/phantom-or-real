# Future Directions — 2026-08-09

## 1. What's new in the literature since the July 4 sweep

### Theme A — The read/write dissociation is now the field's active fault line

- **Hidden Error Awareness in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning: The Signal Is Diagnostic, Not Causal** (Mech-Interp Workshop @ ICML 2026, arXiv May 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09502) — A fourth independent readable-but-not-causal negative: 0.95-AUROC trace probes, and four intervention families *including probe-guided best-of-N* all fail. For us: the gauge half of our story is settled background and must be added to bibliography section A; our working repair now beats not just steering nulls but selection nulls, which raises the value of running gauge-guided BoN ourselves.
- **Perfect Detection, Failed Control** (arXiv June 23 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.24952) — Hallucination detection is perfectly separable, yet the detection direction sits ~83 degrees from the direction along which control works, and — crucially — the cosine gap is *identical before and after instruction tuning* (0.1197 vs 0.1200). For us: the closest published convergence with gauge/lever, and a material update: the "base-vs-post-trained origin is unasked" framing in three of our candidate directions is now false; the pt experiment must be reframed as adjudication between pretraining-origin (this paper) and DPO-origin (2603.21396) accounts.
- **Steerable but Not Decodable: Function Vectors Operate Beyond the Logit Lens** (arXiv April 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02608) — Function vectors steer at >0.90 accuracy while logit lens, tuned lens, and MLP probes fail to decode them at every layer. For us: normalizes our chance-decodable lever as a real regime, and supplies the probe-escalation battery we should run on the rank-8 components before a reviewer asks.
- **Inverted Detection and Control in Steering Vectors** (arXiv Aug 3 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02957) — Highly discriminative directions can steer the *opposite* way; a sign-correction diagnostic improves ITI in 27/30 experiments. For us: a third failure taxonomy (entangled / inverted / inert) — running their ISV diagnostic on our gauge-direction steering arms discriminates "clean null" from "sign-unstable" cheaply.

**Field pulse:** both halves of our dissociation now exist separately in print — readable-and-inert (four times over) and causal-but-unreadable — but no one combines zero-cost erasure of the readable stack with a separately located repair channel in one model, and nobody has our cross-model position/subspace inversion. The moat is intact; the vocabulary is being coined by others fast.

### Theme B — What the gauge actually is

- **Diagnosing Correctness Probes under Self-Judgement Confounding** (arXiv July 18 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16799) — On conflict rows, correctness probes track the model's self-judgement (SJ), not objective correctness (OC); SJ directions transfer across domains, OC directions don't. For us: the cleanest support for reading our deletable gauge as a self-report channel; the causal side (gauge~SJ, lever~OC) is explicitly uninvited territory only we can enter.
- **How LLMs Detect and Correct Their Own Errors: The Role of Internal Confidence Signals** (arXiv April 2026, DeepMind, https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.22271) — Causally validated confidence caching at post-answer-newline tokens, replicated on Gemma-3-27B itself. For us: a candidate positive identity for the gauge (monitoring channel, not production channel) — a much stronger framing than "epiphenomenal."
- **Knowledge Knows, Verbalization Tells** (arXiv July 6 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.05013) — Solvability knowledge and its verbalization are distinct linearly decodable channels; fabrication tracks the verbalization channel. For us: a second independent two-channel factorization that our gauge/lever anatomy can test causally.

**Field pulse:** the interpretation of correctness probes is being actively revised toward "probes read the report channel, not the computation." Our deletability result is the strongest causal evidence for that reading, and the SJ/OC conflict-set experiment is the cheapest way to claim the semantic upgrade before someone else does.

### Theme C — The accuracy-steering race and its rigor bar

- **AdaRAS** (arXiv 2601.19847, surfaced in vetting — not in our July digest) — Failure-prediction-gated steering of reasoning-critical neurons, +13pp on AIME-24/25, explicit no-degradation-on-correct claim, cross-dataset transfer. For us: substantially preempts the gauge-gated deployment loop as a headline and softens any "first cross-task correctness-channel transfer" claim; our surviving differentiators are the causal audit and the constant-on-is-safe result.
- **PRISMS** (arXiv July 31 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00218) — 1-16 MLP neurons detect tool misuse at 0.86-1.00 AUC and the *same* sparse basis steers (+14.2pp). For us: a pointed contrast — in their domain the readable basis IS the lever; whether readability and causality coincide is the open variable our paper should name.
- **Mechanistic Interpretability of Code Correctness via SAEs** (ICLR 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.02917) — Predictor and steering directions are distinct; correct-steering corrupts 14.7% of initially-correct code, motivating gating. For us: the nearest published analog of the split, and our +0.266 benefit from firing on already-correct rows directly contradicts their gating assumption — adopt their net-benefit accounting.
- **FaithSteer-BENCH** (arXiv March 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18329) — Steering methods pervasively show "illusory controllability": prompt-conditional alignment that breaks under mild perturbation. For us: the review bar our repair claim will be held to; the paraphrase stress gate is the one experiment a reviewer can run before we do.

**Field pulse:** detect-then-steer is the dominant 2026 motif, with effects of +3-6pp broad-benchmark and +12-14pp on gated/enriched slices; our +0.25 to +0.40 absolute on held-out failures tops the reported range but on a narrow task. Reporting standards are hardening fast (compute-matched comparisons per 2608.04001; quality-conditioned success per 2607.19364; the AxBench "prompting beats steering" line now contested by 2605.31183) — our tables must adopt this vocabulary preemptively.

### Theme D — Position, prediction, and portability

- **Where's the Plan? Locating Latent Planning** (MechInterp @ ICML 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07984) — Future-rhyme information is decodable at line boundaries in all models tested, but only Gemma-3-27B causally consumes it, around layer 30, via ~5 attention heads. For us: same model, same layer as our lever; independent corroboration of Gemma-concentrates/others-don't, and this group is one experiment away from our consumption-circuit story.
- **Predicting Where Steering Vectors Succeed (LAP)** (arXiv April 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15557) — Training-free A_lin metric predicts diff-of-means steering effectiveness at r = +0.86 to +0.91, with a three-regime framework. For us: the ready-made predictive theory for our concentrated-vs-distributed puzzle; computable from forward passes before any steering run, so regime calls can be registered blind.
- **Subliminal Learning Is Steering Vector Distillation** (arXiv May-June 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00995) — SFT on a steered teacher's outputs distills the steering vector itself into the student (adaptive optimizers required; no cross-model transfer). For us: the mechanistic license for lever-as-teacher training, with three free registrable predictions — and their no-cross-model-transfer finding independently corroborates our Gemma-to-Qwen geometry inversion.

**Field pulse:** site selection (which token, which layer, which stable control point) is repeatedly found to dominate whether steering works — converging on our Qwen position-separation result from three directions (2604.02113's control points, VerifySteer's paragraph boundaries, 2605.07984's boundary tokens). Cross-model transfer is going functional (SAE-matched vectors, 2608.05164; anchor spaces, 2605.09875), but everything ported so far is style/semantic — no causal repair channel has crossed families. Tooling matured for our exact models: Gemma Scope 2 now covers Gemma-3-27B, and the Anthropic workspace paper has a citable arXiv version (2607.15495) that retires our bibliography's UNV flag.

## 2. Can gauge-not-lever become a general accuracy method?

The honest bar first. The strong label-free test-time baselines are no longer vanilla self-consistency: refinement-then-vote (2608.05643), per-instance latent search (LatentSeek, 2505.13308), and probe-guided candidate selection (FPCG, 2606.11172) define the frontier, and 2608.04001 demands compute-matched inference-system accounting for any claim against them. Published accuracy-steering results cluster at +3-6pp on broad benchmarks (CorrSteer MMLU +3.3; control-point selection MATH-500 +5.0) and +12-14pp on gated or failure-enriched slices (AdaRAS +13pp AIME; PRISMS +14.2pp). Our repair numbers — +0.245 on held-out failures, +0.399 answer-free on a 0.12 baseline — sit above that range, but on one synthetic task family, one prompt template, and failure-enriched row sets.

Where latent access plausibly wins is exactly where our baseline table already shows it: sampling-starved failure regimes. On our rows, self-consistency scores 0.000 and best-of-8 only 0.192, because errors are correlated across rollouts — resampling regenerates the same wrong commitment. The lever reaches answers that no amount of prompting-side compute recovers, and 2605.09502's finding that even probe-guided best-of-N fails elsewhere makes a working latent repair rarer than we knew. That is the regime argument for the method: not "steering beats prompting in general" (AxBench's contested claim either way) but "a causal write reaches states that sampling provably cannot."

What +0.399 does *not* license is the word "method." The construction is answer-free in content but not in addressing: gold concept-mention positions are load-bearing (position-free application yields +0.029 n.s.), and item H showed every pre-named answer-free position policy fails — the vector is a positional commitment command, and knowing where to point it is answer-adjacent information we currently get from the oracle. Additionally, the recipe has never left the ontology task, never survived a paraphrase gate, and the Qwen port showed the compression regime (project-then-write) does not transfer even when the raw recipe does. Until addressing is endogenized and the recipe survives at least one external task, the lever is an anatomical instrument with a deployment ledger, not a technique.

Two experiments most change the answer, and they compose. The single highest-leverage one is the self-addressing sweep (Tier 2 below): enumerate candidate concepts from the prompt, fire the lever at each in parallel branches, and let the gauge select the branch — the first true gauge+lever composition, and if it beats compute-matched best-of-K sampling, the loop is answer-free end-to-end and the single blocker to method status falls. The second is the GSM8K transplant with a registered regime prediction: even a predicted null there is a validated scope statement rather than a dead experiment. If both land positive, this is a method paper; if both null, the honest conclusion is that we own a uniquely well-audited diagnostic instrument for one reasoning regime — which is still the paper we are currently writing.
