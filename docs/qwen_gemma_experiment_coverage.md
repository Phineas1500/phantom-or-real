# Qwen vs Gemma Experiment Coverage

This is the working coverage audit for the active goal: mirror the Gemma Stage 2
experiments on Qwen as far as the current Qwen/Qwen Scope artifacts allow.

The scoped completion audit for the 27B target is `docs/qwen_27b_completion_audit.md`.

## Current Status

As of 2026-05-24, Qwen3.5-27B has completed the core Gemma-style behavioral,
L45 raw-probe, residual-SAE, reconstruction/error, forced-choice, patching, and
raw layer-scan passes. The raw layer scan over Qwen layers 16/31/40/53 is
complete for S1 and S3, with L53 strongest for both tasks on both splits. The
matching dtype sanity, L53 Qwen Scope residual-SAE feature extraction, L45/L53
raw free-form steering, answer-property steering, L45/L53 Qwen Scope
sparse-bundle steering, L53 Qwen Scope single-feature steering, L53 local
feature-stability audits, dependent L53 probe/reconstruction diagnostics, raw
Qwen MLP-site probes, residual-only Qwen Scope sparse concat probes, local
learned-dictionary reconstruction/component diagnostics, local sparse-concat
leave-one-block-out ablation, and
local learned-sparse dense-active controls have completed. Local split-trained Qwen L53 `mlp_out`,
`mlp_in_weighted` -> `mlp_out` transcoder-style, and multi-layer
crosscoder-style top-k dictionary pilots are also complete as
non-public-artifact stand-ins for the Gemma learned-dictionary slots.

## Next Decision Points

1. Treat the completed Qwen residual-SAE causal mirror as matching the Gemma
   predictive-versus-causal pattern: L53 improves readout strength, but neither
   bundle nor single-feature Qwen Scope steering exposes control.
2. Treat raw Qwen MLP-site probes, residual-only sparse concat, local
   MLP-output/transcoder/crosscoder pilots, and the residual+local concat as
   completed coverage extenders: they strengthen the predictive-readout
   comparison but do not replace first-party Gemma-style Qwen artifacts.
3. The remaining limitation is artifact provenance rather than local coverage:
   a 2026-05-24 check of the official Qwen-Scope Hugging Face collection still
   lists residual `SAE-Res-*` artifacts rather than MLP-output SAE, transcoder,
   or crosscoder families matching Gemma Scope 2, so the new learned-dictionary
   mirrors must be labeled as local split-trained pilots.
