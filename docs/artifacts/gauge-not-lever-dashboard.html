Phantom or Real? · Stage 2 — Causal Validation · Status July 4, 2026

Gauge, not lever.

Correctness in ontology reasoning is robustly readable from activations — and causally epiphenomenal as a readout. The variable that actually moves behavior is target-concept focus: written at concept-mention tokens during prompt processing, repairable by patching, carried by a compact low-rank core that lives outside the readable subspace.

Headline experiments

Effect on P(strong-correct) versus each run's in-job baseline. Whiskers are 95% row-cluster bootstrap intervals — rows, not generations, are the unit of evidence.

Erasure: the readout axis is not load-bearing

Gemma property · jobs 457207–457210 · 16 rows × k=4 · baseline P(strong) = 0.359

Verdict: raw-axis and height-direction erasure are null; matched orthogonal and Gaussian erasure demolish accuracy. Caveat (pre-registered branch): harmlessness tracks the direction's low within-run projection variance, so the claim is non-necessity of the readout axis, not that it is specially inert among same-norm directions.

The field is closing in — and stopping at the wall we climbed

Deep literature sweep, July 4 (18 primary sources, every load-bearing claim verified 3-vote against the papers). Verdict: not scooped — but three preprints from March–May 2026 independently publish the readable-but-unsteerable half. All three stop at the null.

arXiv 2605.05715 · May 2026

Decodable but not corrected by fixed linear steering

Medical failure regime decodable at 71.6%; 29 steering configs all null. Explains it as entanglement — their LEACE erasure damages accuracy (−3.6pp), the opposite of our harmless erasure. Our readable-stack job (in flight) discriminates the two accounts directly.

arXiv 2604.13068 · Apr 2026

Detection without correction, 7/7 models

Probes detect hallucination signals; steering along the probe direction corrects in 0 of 42 model × magnitude configs, including Qwen-2.5. A self-described clean negative — which also means a Qwen null replication adds nothing; only porting our positive carrier would.

arXiv 2605.23315 · May 2026

Already coined "epiphenomenal correctness"

Correctness decodable across 16 models; ablating top probe components flips only 1.5–5.5% of predictions. A cross-model convergence result — no localization, no repair. Our title wording must cite and differentiate at first use of "epiphenomenal."

The moat: none of the three localizes the causal variable or repairs behavior. The rank-8 concept-focus core with a fresh-row-validated +0.23 repair is the contribution nobody else has — so the paper now leads with the carrier, not the gap, and the niche is crowding fast enough that drafting soon matters.
