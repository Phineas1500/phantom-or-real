Gauge, Not Lever: Dissociating Readable Correctness from its Causal Repair Channel

In a model that visibly predicts its own failures, the readable correctness signal and the causal repair channel are different objects: the entire readable subspace can be deleted without behavioral cost, while repair flows exclusively through a low-rank channel that carries the label-specific content of natural success yet is itself no more decodable than chance — gauge and lever, anatomically separated.

ICLR format · 4–6 pages 16 pre-registered verdicts 2 adversarial review rounds survived every number below: pooled, cluster-bootstrapped

AbstractThe argument in six steps

The gauge. Models linearly encode whether they will answer correctly (AUC ≤ 0.94, two models, scramble-robust) — and the field just showed this readout resists steering.
The gauge is not necessary. Deleting everything linearly readable — rank-9 × 5 layers × every token — costs nothing (+0.05); matched random subspaces are catastrophic (−0.38).
The lever. Correctness is controlled at concept-mention tokens: a rank-8 channel repairs held-out failures (+0.25) and passes every specificity control.
The necessity dissociation. A natural correct-minus-incorrect vector is null at full dimension, potent (+0.34) only when concentrated into the channel — and only with real labels (shuffled ≈ 0, sign-flipped harmful).
The inversion. The channel that causally controls success is itself not decodably privileged (probe inside the random-subspace null) — the mirror image of the readout.
Answer-free repair. A fully donor-free variant reaches +0.40 on a 0.12 baseline (self-consistency: 0.000); firing it on already-correct puzzles helps (+0.27).

§5.5The F-series: what the channel carries

Fig 4 — the signature figure · 26 rows · all CIs pre-registered

Figure 4 — necessity, labels, and answer-free repair in one ladder

One vector, five fates. Full-dimensional: null. Concentrated into the channel: +0.341. Labels scrambled (geometry, norm, positions identical): nothing. Direction flipped: actively harmful. Fully answer-free (direction from watching natural successes, amplitude from pooled scale): the strongest arm in the program. Baselines that no rival reports: majority-vote self-consistency scores 0.000 on these puzzles; even best-of-8 sampling caps at 0.192 — less than half the steered result. Deployment profile: collateral firing on correct puzzles is beneficial (+0.27); writing at all positions cancels (+0.03 n.s.) — addressing is the one remaining answer-adjacent ingredient. Item H closed it: pre-named answer-free position policies fail; the mechanism readout says the vector is a positional commitment command — content is the verb, the marked concept is the object.

§6Discussion commitments

Information location ≠ causal control, in both directions — with consequences for probe-based monitoring and a testable prediction for the steering lineage (the projection-headroom hypothesis: published raw-vector methods pay a dilution tax and a damage ceiling that causal filtering removes). Honesty ledger: endogeneity tested and null at this layer/rank; misdirection asymmetry named as an open anomaly; "an identified sufficient channel," not the unique mechanism; MDE statements on every null; brittleness and entropy telemetry listed as unrun.
