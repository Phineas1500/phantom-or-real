Phantom or Real — Where the Causality Hunt Stands

We could always read whether the model was about to be right. This week we learned why we could never steer it: the thing we were reading turns out to be a gauge, not a lever.

Part 1 · The puzzle we started with

Readable everywhere, steerable nowhere

A simple linear probe on the residual stream predicts whether the model's free-form answer will be correct — at every layer we extract, getting stronger toward the late layers. This readout survives name-scrambling, works on Qwen too, and beats every metadata baseline.

But every attempt to use this signal causally failed: raw-direction steering, optimized vectors, DAS low-rank interchange, exact patching, decode-time gated injection — all zero repairs across the board. For months the honest summary was “causally inaccessible under tested methods.” The new experiments asked the question the other way around.

Part 2 · Experiment 1 — the necessity test

Delete the signal. Does the model care?

Instead of adding the correctness direction (steering), we erased it — clamped each layer's probe direction to its mean value at all five layers at once, at every token position, through prompt processing and decoding. As controls, we did the identical operation to random directions of matched scale. 16 balanced rows × 4 conditions × 8 sampled generations = 512 runs.

Verdict: the correctness readout is causally epiphenomenal. It is a gauge the computation never consults — which is precisely why steering it never repaired anything. Months of nulls became one explained phenomenon.

Part 3 · The loophole check

“But the information is still in there” — yes, and that makes it stronger

Obvious objection: we erased one direction per layer; maybe the model just reads correctness from another direction. The check: erase the axis from stored activations, train a fresh probe on what remains, repeat eight times (INLP-style).

This scopes rather than undermines the verdict: the model kept abundant correctness information through our runtime erasure and still didn't behaviorally depend on the canonical axis. The readout is directionally epiphenomenal and informationally redundant — many needles on the same gauge, none of them load-bearing.

Open scope: erasing the entire redundant subspace (LEACE-style) is a different, heavier experiment — listed as future work, since removing a high-dimensional subspace risks the same off-manifold destruction the orthogonal control demonstrated.
Part 4 · Experiment 2 — hunting the real decision

The model knows the answer. Where is it keeping it?

On the recognition-gap rows, the model generates the wrong hypothesis free-form, yet picks the gold hypothesis when both are shown as options — verified again here: 14 of 14 donor runs chose gold. So a correct-selection state exists in the same model on the same problem. We tried to transplant it: capture the MCQ run's encoding of the shared ~90-token ontology context (layers 30/40/45) and patch it into the free-form run.

A null with localization value: the gold preference is not carried by the context-token encodings. By elimination it lives in the parts of the MCQ prompt that have no free-form counterpart — the explicit hypothesis-option tokens, or the comparison computation over them. That is exactly where the next experiment points.
Part 5 · The behavioral confirmation (new — ran on Modal in 17 seconds)

Give it candidates, and it picks the right one

If the gap is really about candidate availability at selection time, then simply listing candidate hypotheses in the free-form prompt — without forcing a choice — should fix generation on the recognition-gap rows. We ran this black-box test on the Modal endpoint, in parallel with the cluster queue: 14 rows × 3 conditions × 8 samples.

The deployment gap is confirmed behaviorally: the model has the knowledge and the selection machinery — what free-form generation lacks is candidates to select among. The next experiment (option-span interchange) is the activation-level version of exactly this.

So the bottleneck is now localized to a single variable: choosing which concept to hypothesize about. Recognition (14/14), candidate selection (0.839), statement formation (0.955 once the concept is named), and weak self-evaluation (+0.15) all work. Baseline proposals cluster in a small neighborhood (the modal wrong answer takes 58% of 16 samples) that simply never contains gold. target_concept selection during prompt processing is the prime target for the activation-level interchange experiment.

Part 6 · The updated picture

One diagram of what we now believe

In one sentence: correctness is readable everywhere and needed nowhere — the probe reads an epiphenomenal evaluation of a selection process that the tested interventions never touched. The selection process is now the target.

Part 7 · The first positive causal result — property task (subtype is token-routed: Part 9½)

Patching the focus state repairs generation

The interchange test: run the hint-first prompt (which repairs at 1.000 behaviorally) as a donor, capture its hint-conditioned encodings of the ontology context (layers 30/40/45), and patch them into the unhinted run before generating. Does the focus state transfer through activations instead of tokens?

A causal repair handle, confirmed at power. After months in which no correctness-based intervention moved the model, patching the hint-conditioned focus state repairs half the probability mass on rows the model reliably gets wrong — and the effect survived a 4× sample-size increase essentially unchanged.
Two findings that shape what it is: (1) the carrier is predominantly content — the position-shuffled donor retains most of the repair (+0.393 of +0.491), with the ordered patch adding a small but real increment (+0.098, CI [+0.01, +0.21]). That raises the odds a low-rank hint-delta can reproduce the effect, making the add-to-unhinted arm the priority follow-up. (2) Misdirection does not transfer through activations: the wrong-donor arm is null on accuracy (+0.045) and on the concept-resolved metric built to detect it (targets-wrong-concept Δ = +0.018, CI [−0.21, +0.26]) — while the same wrong hint through tokens misdirects completely. Asymmetric controllability: token-reachable and intervention-reachable states diverge. The KV-transplant variant tests whether decode-time attention to literal hint tokens explains the asymmetry.
Part 8 · The decisive experiment's answer — property task

Where the focus state lives — and where it doesn't

Six arms, pre-registered outcome grid, one batched job (456999). The question: is the repair-carrying state a low-rank movable vector, or distributed content — and which positions hold it?

Verdict (pre-registered bin): the focus state is positionally localized — and not captured by a uniform rank-1 summary. It is written at the concept-mention tokens: roughly 6× per-token potency there versus matched-size random positions (+0.250 vs +0.045). Geometry: the block-mean deltas project onto the full 9-direction readable subspace at 0.009–0.021, below the 0.041 chance level — a claim that has since been verified on concept-position-restricted deltas and quantified (Part 9).
The dilution catch: the mean-delta null is diluted, not decisive — averaged over ~90 tokens when the causal content sits at 5–15, with the geometrically shared (and causally inert, pairwise cos 0.23–0.38) component dominating the mean. The arm tested "uniform rank-1 across the block," not "low-rank where it lives." The same dilution overdetermines the cross-row inertness. Sharpened tests — most since run in the composite job (Part 9); the causal rank-k add, the ×2 scale point, and the KV transplant ride the KV job: restricted delta-add with scale sweep, rank-k PCA on the concept-position submatrix, the spotlight test (row A's concept-position delta at row B's concept positions — movable attention operator vs in-place concept commitment), reverse subset patch (necessity: does the hint's 1.000 collapse without its concept-position states?), complement patch, and the KV transplant.

One loop this result closes: the recognition-patch null and the hint-patch success are a single account — MCQ selection happens at option tokens, hints rewrite the context at concept mentions. Each prompt format stores its selection state where its candidates live.

Part 9 · The composite verdict — commitment, written in place, multiply realized (property task)

What lives at the concept mentions — and why no single cut removes it

Nine arms, every pre-registered bin answered (job 457002, 1,008 generations). Left side: the necessity test. Right side: the expressivity ladder and spotlight.

The geometry is now quantified and bulletproof: concept-position-restricted deltas project onto the readable subspace at 0.013–0.014 against an empirical null of 0.040 ± 0.009 — z ≈ −2.8 per delta, with 86% of all 360 per-position deltas individually below the null−2σ line. The causal content is invisible to everything the probes can read.

The discussion's spine — redundancy symmetry: the program has now produced the same lesson at two granularities. Directional necessity failed for the gauge (information redundant across directions: the INLP curve), and positional necessity failed for the lever (commitment redundant across pathways: the hint-token span plus in-place writes). In causal-abstraction terms the variable is multiply realized — interchange sufficiency is the right probe, and exhaustive necessity requires ablating all realizations at once, which is exactly the KV job's combination arm (hint-span decode-masking × concept-position reversion, bins pre-registered).

Part 9½ · The scope finding — on subtype, the old-trio patch is null, but L35 repairs

Same hint, same machinery, different depth

The localization replication on the subtype task landed in its pre-registered task-dependence bin: the full-block hint-state patch is null (+0.039, CI [−0.16, +0.23]; property: +0.491), the concept-subset arm marginal (+0.039, CI [−0.01, +0.12], different rows than full-patch — averaging coincidence verified), the random control clean — despite donors behaviorally validated at 0.875 through tokens on the same 16 rows.

The null survived instrument verification before becoming prose: resolved config read from the run's own log; a 16/16-clean span audit (patched positions decode to the literal concept tokens, donor/receiver token ids exactly aligned); and the delta-norm diagnostic showing the hint writes to subtype context encodings at normal magnitude (L45 norm 8683 vs property's 8058) — the writes are present, they just carry no transferable repair at L30/40/45.

The registered discriminator has now landed: the subtype capture ladder found large late hinted-vs-unhinted writes and selected off-trio layers L53/L50/L35, but targeted causal patches did not deliver a decisive layer-mismatch repair. Old-trio replay stayed null-ish (+0.016); L53/L50 concept replacement were small (+0.031/+0.039); L35 was only suggestive (+0.102, CI crossing zero; matched concept-minus-random +0.109 with the lower CI at zero); and L53 rank-4 LOO add was exactly baseline. The subtype writes are present, but simple residual-state concept-position replacement is not a robust transfer route. Claims 5–6 therefore remain property-scoped, with L35 as a possible replication target rather than a landed subtype causal handle.

Update (July 3): the old-trio null now replicates on two fresh seeds (+0.047 and +0.000, CIs spanning zero — jobs 458376/458377, which ran old-trio-only after sbatch's --export comma-splitting silently dropped the L35 ladder).

And the L35 replication (jobs 458387/458388) fires the pre-registered positive branch: pooled over two fresh seeds (16 rows, k=16), L35 concept replacement repairs +0.117 [CI +0.008, +0.254] with the matched random control null (+0.023) and the old trio null again in-job; the k=24 meta-pool with job 457170 concurs (+0.112 [+0.003, +0.260]). The subtype carrier is reachable at L35 — the old-trio nulls were layer mismatch, not task-level absence of the variable. Caveats carried into the paper: the repair is modest (~a quarter of property's) and row-sparse (3 of 13 addressable rows carry it: 0→0.31, 0→0.62, 0.06→0.94), and the rank-4 LOO add is only marginal (+0.047) — the focus-state variable exists on subtype at a different depth, but no compact core is landed there. Section 5.6 now reads "different layer, weaker handle," not "non-transfer."
Part 9¾ · The fresh-row guard — the compact core is not an artifact of its 13 rows (jobs 458374/458375)

Rank-8 carries 91% of the repair to rows the core never saw

Every prior compact-core number came from the same 13 composite-manifest rows that defined the PCA bases. Guard v2 re-ran the whole design on 26 fresh strong-incorrect h3/h4 rows (seeded selection, composite rows excluded; 1,248 generations, k=8, in-job leave-one-row-out bases), with the pre-registered rule fixed before unblinding: survive if a rank-k LOO CI excludes zero and reaches ≥70% of the in-job concept-replace effect.

Claim 8 survives out-of-distribution, with sharpened wording: the compact causal core transfers to rows disjoint from its entire development history, but rank-8 is the portable basis — rank-4 (77% in-distribution on the original guard) drops to 57% on fresh rows. The paper states a low-dimensional core with useful structure through rank 8, not intrinsic dimension = 4.
